## Source-backed wave-degree rule

1. The 5/34/5 MACD is described as always measuring an Elliott wave; the analytical question is which degree it is measuring.
2. The Course's stated proportionality for the wave sequence under consideration is **100–140 bars**.
3. If the counted sequence is under 100 bars, the indicator is measuring a larger-degree wave; if it is substantially over 140 bars, it is measuring a smaller-degree wave.
4. The example uses a lower timeframe to make the sequence under consideration occupy about 120 bars. This is a scale-selection example, not a universal fixed timeframe.

## Source-backed nested-wave interpretation

- With the 100–140 bar proportion correct, a divergence that does **not** return to the zero line is described as a possible wave-3 peak inside a larger wave 3.
- The described sequence is: MACD retraces but not to zero, returns and generates another divergence; the Course interprets this as wave 3 of wave 3 and places the larger wave-3 peak at that point.
- A later return of MACD to the zero line is described as indicating that the **minimum requirements** for the larger W4 have been met.
- This passage is a specific alternative wave-degree interpretation. It does not support treating every non-zero-return divergence as a confirmed W5 termination.

## Source-backed W5 target construction

1. After W4 is over and W5 is underway, measure the distance from the beginning of W1 to the end of W3.
2. Project that distance from the end of W4 to obtain the 100% reference.
3. Take 62% of that same distance. The Course describes the interval between the 62% and 100% projections as the preferred W5 target zone.
4. Count the five waves inside W5 and repeat the measurement for the internal five-wave sequence to derive a smaller target zone, including the W5-of-W5 subdivision.

This is a target-zone construction, not a guarantee that price reaches the zone and not a substitute for determining whether W4 has actually completed.

## Interpretation guards

- Do not count 100–140 candles in the file and assume that the wave under consideration has the required proportion; the bars must belong to the actual candidate wave sequence.
- Do not interpret a divergence without a return to zero as automatically proving W5; the Course explicitly gives a nested wave-3-of-wave-3 interpretation.
- Do not confuse the zero-line return/minimum-W4 condition with proof that W4 is complete.
- Do not calculate a W5 target from an unverified wave count or a mismatched degree.
- Do not turn the 62–100% target zone into a guaranteed take-profit or expert label.
