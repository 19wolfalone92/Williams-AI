## Source-backed W4 interpretation

1. **Use the correct wave degree first.** The 5/34/5 MACD is described as measuring an Elliott-wave sequence. For the wave sequence being counted, the Course recommends a proportion of **100–140 bars**.
2. **Bar-count direction matters.** If the sequence being counted is shorter than 100 bars, the MACD is measuring a larger-degree wave; if it is substantially longer than 140 bars, it is measuring a smaller-degree wave. The example changes timeframe so the wave under consideration spans about 120 bars.
3. **A histogram below its signal line is not a long-entry confirmation.** During the retracing W4 example, the histogram falls below the signal line; the text explicitly warns that this is not a good place to initiate a long position.
4. **The stated trigger is the oscillator moving past the zero line.** The source describes movement through/past zero, not a mere touch or a bar that only approaches the line.
5. **The trigger marks minimum W4 requirements, not completion.** The Course explicitly says the zero-line trigger does not prove that W4 is over. It says it is premature to place a W5 trade until the indicator has triggered, while also warning that the trigger itself does not establish W4 completion.
6. **Additional structure is required for a more precise W4 assessment.** The text recommends examining an a-b-c or triangle correction and the last smaller wave, and refers to four regular indicators inside that smaller wave. The reviewed page does not enumerate those four indicators in its printed text; handwritten marginal notes are not treated as source rules.

## Interpretation guards

- A zero-line touch, approach or equality is not the stated “moving past” trigger.
- A zero-line crossing is evidence that the Course's minimum W4 requirements have been met; it is **not** proof that W4 is complete.
- A 100–140 bar count applies to the wave sequence being analyzed, not merely to the total amount of chart history loaded.
- The oscillator being below its signal line during the W4 retracement is not itself a reason to initiate a long position.
- Do not convert the zero-line trigger into an unconditional W5 order or a guaranteed wave count.

## What these pages do not establish

- A fully deterministic Elliott-wave counting algorithm or certainty of a particular degree.
- A universal fixed chart timeframe; the example demonstrates changing timeframe to obtain an appropriate bar proportion.
- A complete enumerated definition of the “four regular indicators” on the reviewed page.
- A broker order, fill, risk size, or guaranteed completion point for W4/W5.
